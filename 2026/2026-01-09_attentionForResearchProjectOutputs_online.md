## Plan the journey

* know your audience (i.e. stakeholders, e.g. 'researcher', 'funder')
* look for opportunities to intervene
** 'friction' (bad/ not to do)
** 'accelerators' (good, motivation, encourage)
* develop strategies to intervene (-> communication is key!)
