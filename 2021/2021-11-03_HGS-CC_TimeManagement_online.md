## Managing Time:
* first step for doing better?
 * Understand the time you have
 * Explore own pattern of using time
   * Write a diary: for five days with four columns (from-till; what?; prioritise afterwards; remarks)
   * Analyse after five days and give priorities (1-3)
   * Is there a time-span when things are less exhausting? (add in remarks - exhausting, smooth)
   * Are there any time-thieves? Distraction?
     (formatting? e-mails? check social media)
   * How to handle different prioritised tasks
   * Paper person - App person
   * time tracking apps: 'aTimeLogger' (alternative to diary)
